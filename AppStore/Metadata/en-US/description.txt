Turn rough voice notes into clear text you can actually send.

Whisper Polish records and transcribes on your iPhone. When you want a cleaner version, choose a style—email, text message, Slack update, bullet summary, and more.

• On-device voice transcription
• Keep original and polished versions together
• Optional Whisper Polish Cloud subscription
• Create custom polish styles
• Record voice notes or type directly
• No account required

The app is free. Recording and transcription stay on your iPhone. Subscribe to Whisper Polish Cloud for up to 300 cloud polishes each month.

Whisper Polish Cloud is $4.99 per month in the United States. Pricing may vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel your subscription in App Store settings.
